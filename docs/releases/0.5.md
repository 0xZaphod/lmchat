Adds message editing and keeps replies running when you leave the app or turn off the screen.

- Edit and resend messages, regenerate replies, or delete individual messages from the message menu.
- Keep receiving replies in the background and while the screen is off.
- Editing or regenerating removes later messages after confirmation.
- Improve attachment handling and context counts when changing conversation history.
